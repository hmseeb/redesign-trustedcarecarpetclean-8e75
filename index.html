What We Clean

Complete Carpet & Upholstery Care

One crew, one truck, every soft surface in your home or office. Every job starts with a walk-through and a written flat-rate price — no hidden add-ons once we arrive.

Carpet Steam Cleaning

Truck-mounted hot-water extraction at 210°F lifts embedded soil, dust mites and traffic-lane wear from wall-to-wall carpet, stairs and hallways.

  • Pre-treat & agitate high-traffic lanes
  • Deodorize + optional stain protector
  • Fast 4–6 hour dry time

Upholstery & Sofa Cleaning

Sectionals, recliners, dining chairs and headboards cleaned with fabric-matched solutions — from durable microfiber to delicate linen and velvet.

  • Fiber test before any solution touches fabric
  • Body-oil and food-soil removal on arms & cushions
  • Low-moisture method for delicate weaves

Area & Oriental Rug Cleaning

Wool, silk-blend and hand-knotted rugs get gentle, pH-balanced care with full color-fastness testing before a drop of water is applied.

  • Dry-soil removal on both faces
  • Fringe detailing & controlled drying
  • In-home or pick-up & delivery

Pet Odor & Stain Removal

We find the source with UV inspection, then flush the padding and sub-floor with enzyme treatment that destroys odor instead of masking it.

  • UV black-light contamination mapping
  • Sub-surface enzyme flush & extraction
  • Safe around pets and children once dry

Tile & Grout Restoration

High-pressure rotary cleaning pulls years of grease and grey haze out of grout lines in kitchens, entryways, bathrooms and patios.

  • Hard-water and soap-scum breakdown
  • Optional clear or color grout sealing
  • Splash-guarded — no mess on your walls

Mattress & Allergen Care

A dust-mite and allergen treatment for the surface you spend a third of your life on — ideal for allergy sufferers and South Texas cedar season.

  • HEPA dry-soil extraction
  • Anti-allergen sanitizing rinse
  • Ready to sleep on the same night

How It Works

Four Simple Steps to a Fresh Home

No surprises, no upselling at the door. Here's exactly what your appointment looks like.

  1. 01

    Call or Request a Quote

    Tell us your rooms, fabrics and problem spots. You get a flat-rate price over the phone in about five minutes.

  2. 02

    Walk-Through & Pre-Treat

    Your technician texts ahead, inspects every area with you, protects corners and pre-treats spots and traffic lanes.

  3. 03

    Deep Extraction

    Truck-mounted hot-water extraction rinses out soil and solution together, then we groom the fibers to stand back up.

  4. 04

    Final Inspection

    We walk it again together. If a spot resists, we retreat it on the spot — or come back free within 14 days.
